Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services

What documents do I need for island tours?

For island tours (Inhaca, Xefina) no passport or special visa is required for Mozambican citizens. Foreign tourists need a valid passport. We recommend bringing sunscreen, swimwear, a towel and money for personal expenses.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 48 hours in advance for day tours, and 5 to 7 days for safaris. During peak season (June to October), it is advisable to book 1 to 2 weeks in advance. Transfers can be booked with 24 hours notice.

Are tours carried out in bad weather?

Our clients' safety is our priority. In case of bad weather (strong winds, cyclones or rough seas), the tour is cancelled and the amount paid is fully refunded or rescheduled at no extra cost.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations more than 48 hours in advance: full refund. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before the trip date are non-refundable.

Do prices include transport to the Marina?

Prices include boat transport from Maputo Marina to the destination and back. Land transport to the Marina is not included, but we offer private transfer from your hotel to the Marina at an additional cost.

Are there tours available for children and families?

Yes! We have tours designed for families with children. Minimum ages: snorkelling 8 years, safaris 6 years, boat tours no restriction (life jacket provided). Children aged 4–10 benefit from a reduced fare; children under 4 participate free of charge on most tours.
