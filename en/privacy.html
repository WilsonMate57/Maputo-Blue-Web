Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026.

This policy explains which personal data Maputo Blue collects through this website, why we use it and how you can exercise your rights.

Data we collect

  • Booking requests: name, e-mail, phone, country (optional), date, number of guests, selected options and special requests.
  • Custom tour requests: the contact details and travel preferences you provide.
  • Customer account: name, e-mail, password (stored hashed, never in plain text), optional phone and country.
  • Reviews: the name you enter, your rating and comment, which are shown publicly on the experience page.
  • Security: minimal technical records (for example, a hashed summary of your IP address) to prevent abuse and spam.

How we use it

  • To handle and confirm bookings, prepare quotations and contact you about your trip.
  • To send service e-mails (confirmations, changes, reminders). We do not send marketing without your consent.
  • To manage loyalty benefits linked to your account.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

Sharing

Data is stored on Maputo Blue's servers. Service e-mails are delivered through an e-mail delivery provider. We only share with partners (for example, the lodge or boat operator) the information needed to deliver the service you booked.

Retention

We keep booking data for as long as needed to deliver the service and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Requests that did not become bookings and inactive accounts can be deleted on request.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, by writing to maputoblue.info@gmail.com. You can update your profile at any time in your account.

Cookies

We only use one technical session cookie, needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.
